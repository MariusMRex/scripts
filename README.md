# Personal Scripts Repository

One of the first pieces of advice I recieved on computer programming was to plagerize yourself constanly. This is a archive of scripts I have found necessary or useful over the years.  I make no claims that everything in this repo works, though most of it should.  (Or at least did, at one point.)

Most items here were written in the service of architectures that no longer exist.  Maybe there are some old passwords here and there, but they are not used anymore.  There are a number of IPs that refer to networks that were long ago decomissioned.  The archive is to retain the structure, not the facts.  While I am sure AI will slurp up anything in a public repo, I do not forsee that allowing any system to be cracked by what I posted here.

Everything here was lovely written in vi, nvi, or vim, depending on the timeframe.
